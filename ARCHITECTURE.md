# 🏥 AyuCare - Smart Elderly Care & Medication Management Platform
## Master Hackathon Blueprint & Winning Architecture

### 🌟 Problem Statement Alignment & Beyond-Basic Innovation

| Parameter | Mandatory Requirement | AyuCare Winning Innovation (Beyond Basic) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Shared Ecosystem** | Passcode-based multi-role access (Elderly, Caretaker, Family) | **4-Digit Quick PIN for Elderly** + Master Passcode for Caregivers + Unified Family Circle sync |
| **Medication Schedule** | Add, edit, manage schedules & frequencies | **Gemini Vision OCR:** Scan prescription / medicine box photo & auto-generate schedule |
| **Adherence Tracking** | Taken / Missed / Skipped logs | **One-Tap Giant Button** + Streak tracking + Caregiver Adherence % Analytics Chart |
| **Proactive Alerts** | Email reminders for upcoming & missed doses | **3-Tier Escalation Engine:** In-app audio chime → Caregiver Email alert → Red SOS Flag |
| **Accessibility** | Usable by elderly individuals | **WCAG AAA Compliance:** High contrast mode, 3-level font zoom, Voice Readout in Marathi/Hindi/English |
| **Health Safety** | Doctor appointments & healthcare routines | **Gemini Drug-Drug Interaction Checker** + Daily Vital Logging (BP, Sugar, Mood Check-in) |
